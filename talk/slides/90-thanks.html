What it may call

Ring 2, on every call. A verified token, a hard ceiling, then the decision service.

The ruleagentgateway

      || mcp.methodName != "tools/call"
      || mcp.tool.name != "execute_payment"

          && mcp.tool.arguments.amount <= 50000)'
mcpGuardrails:
  processors:
  - kind: remote              # the decision service

Backup · step 7

It cannot rewrite its own bounds

The keystone. Authority changes through a reviewed commit, never at runtime.

The ruleKyverno

# deploy/platform/kyverno/keystone-policy.yaml
apiVersion: policies.kyverno.io/v1
kind: ValidatingPolicy
metadata:
  name: agents-cannot-edit-their-own-authority
spec:
  validationActions: [Deny]
  matchConstraints:
    resourceRules:
    - resources: ["featureflags", "featureflagsources"]
    # … and RBAC, policies, routes, SPIFFE IDs, workloads
  matchConditions:
  - expression: >-
      request.userInfo.username.startsWith(
        'system:serviceaccount:northstar-agents:') || …
