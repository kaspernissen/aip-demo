Guardrails for model traffic

What agentgateway v1.6.0 ships · crates/agentgateway/src/llm/policy/

  • Regex rules mask or reject text that matches
  • Personal data is found and masked: card numbers, emails, phone numbers, national IDs
  • Moderation asks a moderation model to judge the prompt
  • A webhook lets your own service judge the prompt and the answer
  • Connectors call Amazon Bedrock Guardrails, Azure Content Safety or Google Model Armor

Northstar’s model calls go through the gateway. None of these guards are switched on.
