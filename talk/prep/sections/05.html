What the slide is for

The agenda, in three verbs. It returns four times: here as the overview, as the section opener for each half (slides 6, 20, 33) with a "DONE" marker on the parts already covered, and as the recap (slide 37). The idea underneath

Decide without Trace is a rule nobody can show was applied. Trace without Decide is a detailed record of an agent doing whatever it liked. Prove is the step regulators and risk teams actually consume: a grade or a verdict built from the record, not a claim from the team that wrote the code. The order matters: the decisions are designed so that each one emits a span, which is why "evidence is a byproduct" (slide 10) is possible at all.
