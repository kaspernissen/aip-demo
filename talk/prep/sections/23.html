If someone asks

Is the model call real?
Yes. Both agents call Claude through agentgateway's llm listener; the key is mounted in the gateway, not in the agents.
What if the customer lies believably under the limit?
The platform can't catch that: the model reads the claim as written. That is fraud detection, outside these guardrails. AgentEvals flags explicit instructions, not plausible lies.
Who is the reviewer, really?
A name typed into a form. There is no login, second approver, timeout or expiry on the reviewer-ui. It is a known limit of the demo.
