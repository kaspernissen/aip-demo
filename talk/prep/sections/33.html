What the slide is for

The distinction to hold for the next three slides

Decide governs what the agent may do, before the action, enforced by the platform. Prove grades what the agent did inside those bounds, after the fact, from the trace. Evaluation never blocks anything in Northstar: by the time AgentEvals runs, the claim is already paid or held. That is deliberate. Blocking belongs in the deterministic rings; evaluation finds behaviour the rings permit but nobody designed (a retry after a refusal, an amount mismatch, a customer trying to instruct the system).

Plain analogy: the rings are the bank's transaction limits; Prove is the internal audit that reviews the transactions afterwards.
